WoundDelta

Is this wound better than last week? Answered by subtraction, not diagnosis. The change is measured in code. A language model only phrases the numbers.

1. Ghost overlay capture guide

Pick the previous photo, then the new one. The previous photo sits at 50% opacity over the new one: that ghost is the capture guide. In the clinic or at home, line the wound up with its ghost before accepting the photo. Same angle, same distance, same light.

2. Or try the synthetic demo

These are the same generated fixtures the test suite measures against known ground truth.

⚠️ Proof of concept — not clinically tested. This tool is for research and software demonstration only. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, or rule out any condition, and its outputs may be wrong, including false positives and false negatives. Do not use it to make decisions about your health. For any medical issue, seek a licensed healthcare professional. In an emergency, go to your nearest emergency room or dial 911 immediately.
