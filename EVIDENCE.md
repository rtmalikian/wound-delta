# Evidence base — WoundDelta

Every load-bearing claim in the README traces to a row below: claim → full
citation → evidence tier → what it proves / does not prove. Tiers follow the
project evidence standard: Tier 1 = systematic review / meta-analysis /
guideline / authoritative compendium; Tier 2 = RCT / large cohort / validated
measurement study on real patients; Tier 3 = small observational / pilot;
Tier 4 = vendor / press / synthetic results, labelled as such and never
dressed as independent evidence.

All citations below were opened and checked on 2026-10-31. This file records
an evidence audit of the claims, not clinical validation of the app:
WoundDelta's own real-world efficacy is unproven until a labelled validation
set exists (roadmap issue #1).

## This app's own numbers

The eval figures quoted in the README (area-change MAE 0.07 percentage
points; red-flag recall 10/10 on synthetic fixtures) are **Tier 4** by
definition: the fixtures are generated by the same code family that measures
them, so they prove pipeline correctness only — never clinical accuracy.

## Red-flag thresholds

The red-flag constants (area +25% since last photo, spreading redness, new
darkening, no improvement over 14 days) are **app-defined triage thresholds,
not diagnostic criteria**. Enlargement and spreading erythema are recognised
deterioration signs, but +25% is an app choice (no published threshold was
found), and 14 days is not the definition of a stalled wound — the
literature's best-validated benchmark is the 4-week rule (failure to achieve
~40–50% area reduction by 4 weeks predicts non-healing). The app thresholds
are deliberately earlier and more sensitive than that benchmark, so they
will over-trigger by design.

## Known gaps

1. **Automated segmentation on patient-taken home photos: no validated
   study found.** The closest studies validate human tracing (Biagioni
   2021) or the method class on pre-2014 literature (Jørgensen 2016) — not
   unsupervised phone segmentation of photos patients take at home.
   Closing this needs Bland–Altman agreement of WoundDelta vs ImageJ /
   clinician tracing on a consecutive real-patient photo set, with
   pre-registered exclusion rules (angle, distance, marker, skin tone,
   wound-edge diffuseness), reported by wound size and skin tone.
2. **Red-flag thresholds (+25%, 14 days): no guideline threshold found.**
   Closing this needs a prospective cohort linking photo-change thresholds
   to clinician-confirmed infection / deterioration, reporting
   sensitivity/specificity at 14 days vs the established 4-week rule.
3. **Home follow-up proportion: no US national estimate found** for the
   share of chronic-wound follow-up managed at home vs clinic, which is
   why the README says "a substantial share" and not "most."
